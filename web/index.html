Features

Everything a road crew needs, in one app

No more group-text chaos. RideSync turns scattered calls, texts and receipts into one clean, live view of your trip.

Live Group Map

See every member move in real time. Know who's ahead, who's lagging, and exactly where to regroup.

Smart Navigation

Turn-by-turn directions with your group's position overlaid. Drive together without getting split up.

Group Chat

One threaded chat per group with live updates. Road reports, photo drops and "where are you?" — solved.

Split Expenses

Log fuel, tolls and meals. RideSync tracks who paid what so settling up at the end is painless.

Photo Drive

A shared album for the trip. Every member's shots land in one place automatically as you ride.

One-Tap SOS

An emergency alert with your live location pings the whole crew and your saved contacts instantly.
